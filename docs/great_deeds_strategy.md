# Great Deeds — Core Strategy & Vision

## 1. The "Family Champion" & onboarding strategy
The app is adopted **one family at a time**, not one user at a time. Each family has a
**Family Champion** — the person who finds the app, creates the Circle, and recruits everyone else.

**Their first experience is the single most critical flow in the product.** If it is confusing,
or their family doesn't join, the whole group is lost.

- **Flawless invitation flow.** Admin → invite → share link. Dead simple, one tap to copy/share.
- **A "Welcome Kit" for invitees.** Not a bare link: what this is, **who invited you (with photo)**,
  and why it is private.
- **First-run experience.** Never show a new joiner an empty feed. Show the Family Tree with
  *them* and their inviter highlighted, plus one tiny task: "Add your birthday."

## 2. The business model must equal trust
The premise is privacy. An ad model requires exploiting user data and would destroy the premise.
**No ads. No data sale. Ever.** Revenue must come from the value we add.

**Freemium subscription.** Core app free for every family member: join Circle, view/add to tree,
view feed, add deeds within a storage allowance.

**Family Premium** — one annual plan covering the whole Circle, usually paid by the Champion:
- Digital **Heirloom Exports** (the killer feature — a "forever" artifact).
- Large storage (free ~2–5GB → premium 100GB+) for high-res photo and video.
- AI features: colorization, enhancement, Story Assistant.
- Automated archive discovery ("Famous Deeds").
- Advanced calendar (external sync, event planning).

## 3. Digital legacy & data permanence
Users are entrusting us with irreplaceable data. Two questions they *should* ask:
"What if the app shuts down?" and "What happens to my stories when I die?"

- **Legacy Contact.** A user designates a family member who can manage their stories or download
  their archive after they pass. Thoughtful, and eventually necessary.
- **Data-portability promise.** Full export is always available, on every tier, publicly promised.
  Not just a feature — a no-lock-in guarantee.

## 4. Content moderation: the "positive vibe" keeper
Families have complex dynamics. One unresolved conflict can make a family abandon the app.

- **Admin-led, private moderation.** No public report buttons that create drama.
  Any member can quietly **Flag for Admin Review**; only Admins are notified.
- **Empower the Admin** with three simple actions: privately message the poster, hide the post,
  or delete the post.
- **Positive reinforcement.** Prompts and copy always steer toward celebratory, shared memories.

## 5. Bridging digital and physical
The app should make the family look *up* from their phones.

- **Print integration.** One tap turns the digital Family Book into a physical hardcover via a
  print-on-demand partner. The perfect gift.
- **Conversation starters.** "On this day 25 years ago your parents got married. Give them a call!"
- **Event-driven UX.** An upcoming reunion changes the home screen: countdown, plus
  "Share your favourite memory from the last reunion."
